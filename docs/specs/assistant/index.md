**F-001 is at revision 4** (2026-08-18, T-103). The conversation surface no longer renders the task list, so **AC-1, AC-4 and AC-15 are restated** — each guarantee unchanged, each mechanism moved onto the turn's own message — and **AC-31 / AC-32 are new**, carrying the two things the restatement would otherwise have dropped (a message is a door to the task it changed; a rendered list is not stale after a turn). AC-18, AC-24 and AC-25 gain notes rather than new substance. Nothing was renumbered or deleted; the count is now **32 ACs**, which `F-003 ## Parity` still enumerates as 29 and is owed an update. The load-bearing constraint throughout is the owner's: **the message carries its full diff at every width — one mechanism, not two** (`docs/reports/owner-decision-2026-08-17-desktop-list-is-primary.md`). The surfaces the list moved *to* are deliberately unspecced here and need their own F-id. **It is now at revision 6** (2026-08-18): rev 5 (T-145) amended AC-24's reachability sub-bullet in place once `F-005 AC-45` put the task detail in the list's column, and rev 6 (T-153) **closes Open Questions 10 and 11** against two owner decisions of the same date — the detail never traps the user (settled by narrowing `F-005 AC-2`, not AC-24) and a message's task-name door **swaps** an open detail's subject (AC-31's route enumeration gains the entry; its postcondition is unamended). **The count is still 32 ACs** — neither revision added one. **It is now at revision 7** (2026-08-19, T-157 absorbing T-140), which amends **AC-31's activatability gate** in place: the door was gated on the task being in the collection **currently shown**, and it is now gated on **the task still existing** — when the collection on screen does not hold the row, activating the door **switches to a collection that does**. The deleted task stays inert for its own reason (no row exists to reach), and the postcondition is again unamended. It matters because `DEFAULT_COLLECTION` is Today and the assistant creates dateless tasks in Inbox, so under the old gate **the task the assistant had just created was the one task in the message that was not a door** — which also made `F-005 AC-48`'s swap route dead for the common case (`F-005 ## Impact §14`). The condition binds **both** client predicates, web's `canReveal` and mobile's `taskLinkState`, because amending one leaves the door meaning two different things on two clients (**L-005**). **Still 32 ACs** — nothing renumbered, deleted or added; what the direction does not settle (which collection, when several hold the task) is **Open Question 12**.

**F-002 is written** (2026-08-17, **revision 3** — the closing revision after Gate 1 round 2; the round cap is 2 and is reached, so revision 3 closes the gate **unreviewed**). It discharges the reservation this row previously held: talk-back / speech output (UC-20) was the binding commitment from F-001's Gate 1 decision D1, and it lands on both clients at once. It carries UC-20's minimal scope — reads, can be turned off, no barge-in and no resume-where-cut. **Three product-owner decisions dated 2026-08-17 are written into it**: talk-back is content rather than incidental sound (the ringer does not silence it), iOS Safari's gesture refusal is surfaced, and a spoken destructive confirmation must name the tasks it will delete. Two consequences of those decisions were not part of the questions asked and remain open in the spec (OQ1b, OQ6). Downstream: `docs/design/_shared/components.md ## Spoken frames` carries the frame catalogue's IDs and slot contracts with its **wording not yet written** — design-agent owns it, and F-002 AC-22's parse test depends on it.

**F-005 is at revision 4** (2026-08-19, T-159 — the final amend pass, taken after the owner **closed Gate 1**; there is no fourth review). **Revision 3** (2026-08-18, T-154 — the Gate 1 round-2 revision) is described below. **Revision 2** (T-143) is described next, and its record stands. It is **the first feature in this repo that changes the `task` model** — `note`, sub-tasks, recurrence, `priority` narrowed from a free string to four values, and now `completed_by_parent`, `reminder_shown_at` and a `deleted_at` that something can finally clear. It is item **#5** on `docs/specs/_shared/uc-coverage-map.md`'s ordered plan and carries **UC-27, UC-44, UC-35, UC-34, UC-36 and UC-39** in full, plus **UC-33 entire** (revision 2: the list row's delete gains the undo it never had) and the in-app half of **UC-26** (a passed reminder is shown when the app opens — no scheduler, no push, no permission prompt). **UC-04** and **UC-18** are excluded with reasons; **UC-49** (a start date, which the owner named) stays excluded pending an owner answer.

**Revision 3 (T-154) closes Gate 1 round 2, and its constraint is the interesting part.** Round 2 put **nine** lenses on **48** ACs — eleven of which no lens had ever read, because the revision-1 fix *added* them — and returned **REJECT: 40 HIGH · 44 MEDIUM · 15 LOW, 99 findings**. The owner **waived the round cap once**, with a rule that is the substance of the waiver rather than a footnote: **revision 3 amends existing acceptance criteria only and adds none** (`docs/reports/owner-decision-2026-08-18-round-three-and-offline.md`). The mechanism that produced round 2 is *a revision adds ACs → the new ACs are unreviewed → the next round finds them*, and no-new-ACs is what makes this the last round instead of the third of an unknown number. **It held: still 48 ACs, nothing renumbered, nothing deleted, nothing added**, and all 99 findings are dispositioned in `docs/reports/gate1-lenses/F-005-revision-3-log.md` — which is also what determines the **targeted** re-review's dispatch set, since each lens re-reads only the ACs it raised findings on. The owner's second answer of the same date is folded in: **an offline field edit is refused, with your value kept on screen and the reason stated** — no queue, no durable store, no replay — which is AC-2's third state and closes **OQ6**, whose premise was itself false (`toggleTask`, `editTask` and `removeTask` all return before attempting anything while offline, so there was never a pending edit whose durability was in question; the app accepted the change and quietly threw it away). Three findings are worth knowing without opening the spec: **close-then-fail** — the ordinary order in an outage — was covered by neither AC-2 nor AC-47, so the trade the owner had just made bought nothing on the most likely close; **AC-44's clock** displaced the problem onto the zone, which travels with a turn while every computation it names is triggered by a task write, and asserted the seam rather than the outcome; and two revision-2 directives were **true as edits and false as outcomes** — *"pick an accent from unspent tokens"* names an empty set, and the seed path three ACs depend on was a sentence with no mechanism and no owner. Four `resolved` rows in the revision-2 log did not land and are corrected there. **Two dependencies leave the spec**: `F-001 AC-31`'s activatability gate (`## Impact` §14 — the swap route is dead for the common case until F-001 amends it), and F-002's wording for a turn-path refusal, which has no `TurnOutcome` member to be returned in today.
