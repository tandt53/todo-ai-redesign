**F-001 is at revision 4** (2026-08-18, T-103). The conversation surface no longer renders the task list, so **AC-1, AC-4 and AC-15 are restated** — each guarantee unchanged, each mechanism moved onto the turn's own message — and **AC-31 / AC-32 are new**, carrying the two things the restatement would otherwise have dropped (a message is a door to the task it changed; a rendered list is not stale after a turn). AC-18, AC-24 and AC-25 gain notes rather than new substance. Nothing was renumbered or deleted; the count is now **32 ACs**, which `F-003 ## Parity` still enumerates as 29 and is owed an update. The load-bearing constraint throughout is the owner's: **the message carries its full diff at every width — one mechanism, not two** (`reports/owner-decision-2026-08-17-desktop-list-is-primary.md`). The surfaces the list moved *to* are deliberately unspecced here and need their own F-id.

**F-002 is written** (2026-08-17, **revision 3** — the closing revision after Gate 1 round 2; the round cap is 2 and is reached, so revision 3 closes the gate **unreviewed**). It discharges the reservation this row previously held: talk-back / speech output (UC-20) was the binding commitment from F-001's Gate 1 decision D1, and it lands on both clients at once. It carries UC-20's minimal scope — reads, can be turned off, no barge-in and no resume-where-cut. **Three product-owner decisions dated 2026-08-17 are written into it**: talk-back is content rather than incidental sound (the ringer does not silence it), iOS Safari's gesture refusal is surfaced, and a spoken destructive confirmation must name the tasks it will delete. Two consequences of those decisions were not part of the questions asked and remain open in the spec (OQ1b, OQ6). Downstream: `design/_shared/components.md ## Spoken frames` carries the frame catalogue's IDs and slot contracts with its **wording not yet written** — design-agent owns it, and F-002 AC-22's parse test depends on it.

**F-005 is written** (2026-08-18, revision 1) and is **the first feature in this repo that changes the `task` model** — `note`, sub-tasks, recurrence, and `priority` narrowed from a free string to four values. It is item **#5** on `specs/_shared/uc-coverage-map.md`'s ordered plan and carries **UC-27, UC-44, UC-35, UC-34, UC-36 and UC-39** in full, plus the detail half of UC-33; **UC-04** (AI decomposition) and **UC-18** (a mic on the task) are excluded with reasons, and **UC-49** (a start date, which the owner named) is excluded pending an owner answer, because the source declines to write ACs for it and UC-39 refuses the series a separate start field on purpose. **37 ACs, web and api; the phone client is not in it** — F-003's precedent, and Open Question 8 puts the call to the owner rather than assuming it. The spec carries an **`## Impact on what already exists`** section, written to `LEARNINGS.md` **L-013**: the task's fields are enumerated at **thirteen sites** in `src/**`, five of which gate behaviour, and the sharpest of them (`apply.ts:28 DIFF_FIELDS`) is simultaneously the assistant's write allowlist and the source of `turn.diff`, so silence there produces an F-001 **AC-4** failure — a message that names a task and cannot say what changed. Ten open questions; three came out of the impact pass. **This is a re-implementation, not a new idea** — `specs/_source/todo-ai/04-feature-audit.md` marks all six in-scope UCs ✅ Done in the product being redesigned, and reading it changed the spec: it supplied the missing title AC (AC-37), overturned a decision the first draft had invented (completing a parent **cascades** to its steps — AC-19), and contributed four shipped failure records to the ACs that would otherwise have re-earned them.

**F-004 is reserved, not committed** — for the spoken day summary (UC-20 AC-20.6), "what's on today" read from local data, no model, working offline. It is excluded from F-002 with reasons. Product-agent's Gate 1 finding is that F-002's spoken surface is exactly empty offline and that F-004 is what would carry that leg of ADR-11's market position — **a finding, not a product-owner decision.** Unlike F-002, which F-001's Gate 1 decision D1 made binding in writing, **nothing commits anyone to building F-004**; whether and when it happens is an open call for the owner. The id is reserved only so it is not reused.
